📖 Articles

A

Use A before a consonant sound.

A boy.
A cat.
A book.

An

Use An before a vowel sound.

An apple.
An elephant.
An umbrella.

The

Use The for a particular person, place or thing.

The sun.
The moon.
The book on the table.
