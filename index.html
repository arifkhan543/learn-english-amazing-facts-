📚 English Vocabulary

Happy

Meaning: Feeling pleasure or joy.

Example: She is happy today.

Brave

Meaning: Not afraid of danger.

Example: He is a brave boy.

Beautiful

Meaning: Very attractive or pleasing.

Example: The garden is beautiful.

Important

Meaning: Having great value.

Example: Education is important.
