📚 Articles: A, An and The

A

Use A before a singular word beginning with a consonant sound.

A boy.
A cat.
A book.

An

Use An before a vowel sound.

An apple.
An egg.
An orange.

The

Use The for a particular person, place or thing.

The sun is hot.
The book is on the table.
Practice: ___ apple, ___ boy, ___ sun.

👤 Pronouns

A pronoun is used instead of a noun.

Rahul is a boy. He is a boy.
Rina is a girl. She is a girl.

⏰ Tense

Tense tells us when an action happens.

Present

I play football.
She reads a book.

Past

I played football.
She read a book.

Future

I will play football.
She will read a book.

⚡ Verbs: V1, V2 and V3

Go → Went → Gone
Eat → Ate → Eaten
Write → Wrote → Written
See → Saw → Seen

V1

Base form: go, eat, write

V2

Past form: went, ate, wrote

V3

Past participle: gone, eaten, written

🔤 Am / Is / Are

I am a student.
He is happy.
She is a teacher.
They are friends.
  • I → am
  • He → is
  • She → is
  • It → is
  • You → are
  • We → are
  • They → are

📝 Has / Have / Had

I have a book.
You have a pen.
He has a car.
She has a bag.
They have a house.

Had

I had a bicycle.
She had a doll.

❓ Do / Does / Did

Do you play football?
Does he play football?
Did you go there yesterday?
  • I / You / We / They → Do
  • He / She / It → Does
  • Past → Did
