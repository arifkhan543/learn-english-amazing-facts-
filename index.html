📚 Articles: A, An and The

A

Use A before a singular word that begins with a consonant sound.

A boy.
A cat.
A book.

An

Use An before a singular word that begins with a vowel sound.

An apple.
An egg.
An orange.

The

Use The when talking about a particular person, place or thing.

The sun is hot.
The book is on the table.
Practice: ___ apple, ___ boy, ___ sun.

👤 Pronouns

A pronoun is a word used instead of a noun.

Rahul is a boy. He is a boy.

Common Pronouns

  • I
  • You
  • He
  • She
  • It
  • We
  • They
Example: Rina is a girl. She is a girl.

📍 Prepositions

Prepositions show the relationship between words.

The book is on the table.
The cat is under the table.
He is in the room.
She is at school.

Common Prepositions

  • in
  • on
  • at
  • under
  • over
  • between
  • behind
  • beside
  • with
  • from
  • to

⏰ Tense

Tense tells us when an action happens.

Present Tense

I play football.
She reads a book.

Past Tense

I played football.
She read a book.

Future Tense

I will play football.
She will read a book.

⚡ Verbs

A verb usually shows an action or state.

Go → Went → Gone
Eat → Ate → Eaten
Write → Wrote → Written
See → Saw → Seen

V1

Base form: go, eat, write

V2

Past form: went, ate, wrote

V3

Past participle: gone, eaten, written

🔤 Am / Is / Are

I am a student.
He is happy.
She is a teacher.
They are friends.

Remember

  • I → am
  • He → is
  • She → is
  • It → is
  • You → are
  • We → are
  • They → are
