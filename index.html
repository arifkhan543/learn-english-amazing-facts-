📚 Articles: A, An and The

A

Use A before a singular word with a consonant sound.

A boy.
A cat.
A book.

An

Use An before a singular word with a vowel sound.

An apple.
An egg.
An orange.

The

Use The for a particular person, place or thing.

The sun is hot.
The book is on the table.
Practice: ___ apple, ___ boy, ___ sun.

👤 Pronouns

A pronoun is used instead of a noun.

Rahul is a boy. He is a boy.
Rina is a girl. She is a girl.

⏰ Tense

Tense tells us when an action happens.

Present: I play football.

Past: I played football.

Future: I will play football.

  • He → is
  • She → is
  • It → is
  • You → are
  • We → are
