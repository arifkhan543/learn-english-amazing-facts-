📖 Daily Vocabulary

🌟

Beautiful

Meaning: সুন্দর

Example: She has a beautiful smile.

💪

Brave

Meaning: সাহসী

Example: He is a brave boy.

🤝

Honest

Meaning: সৎ

Example: He is an honest person.

😊

Happy

Meaning: সুখী

Example: I am very happy today.

🎯

Important

Meaning: গুরুত্বপূর্ণ

Example: English is important.

🚀

Improve

Meaning: উন্নতি করা

Example: I want to improve my English.

🗣️ Spoken English

👋 Greeting

English: How are you?

Answer: I am fine, thank you.

☀️ Morning

English: Good morning!

Answer: Good morning! How are you?

🙏 Thank You

English: Thank you very much.

Answer: You're welcome.

👋 Goodbye

English: See you later.

Answer: See you!

❓ Asking

English: What are you doing?

Answer: I am studying English.

💬 Opinion

English: What do you think?

Answer: I think it is good.
